Pomodoro Timer

Classic 25-minute focus sessions with 5-minute breaks, fully customizable. A clean countdown display and audible alerts signal when to work and when to rest — no guessing needed.

Distraction Blocker

Activates a minimal focus environment during sessions to reduce visual noise and resist the urge to context-switch. Stay locked in for the full Pomodoro without losing momentum.

Task Prioritization

Add tasks before each session and mark which one to tackle in each Pomodoro. Priority sorting ensures you work on the highest-impact items first, not just the most comfortable ones.

Session Analytics

A daily and weekly dashboard shows completed Pomodoros, total focus hours, tasks finished, and your most productive time of day — helping you optimize your schedule over time.

Break Reminders

When a session ends, the app actively reminds you to take your break. Stepping away between Pomodoros is as important as the focused work — the reminder makes sure you actually do it.

Focus Music

Built-in ambient soundscapes and focus playlists play during work sessions. Choose from lo-fi beats, white noise, nature sounds, or silence — whatever helps you reach flow state fastest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Pomodoro Focus Timer template free to use?
Yes. You can start building your Pomodoro focus timer for free on MeDo — no credit card or coding skills required. MeDo handles hosting and deployment automatically, so there are no server costs to get started.
How does the distraction blocker work?
During active focus sessions, the distraction blocker creates a minimal full-screen environment that hides notifications and reduces visual clutter. It helps you maintain attention on the current task for the full Pomodoro interval without external interruptions.
Can I customize the Pomodoro session length?
Yes. While the classic Pomodoro technique uses 25-minute work sessions and 5-minute breaks, you can fully customize session duration, break lengths, and the number of Pomodoros before a long break. Through MeDo's AI interface you can tailor every timing parameter to your workflow.
Does the app track how many focus sessions I complete?
Yes. The session analytics dashboard records completed Pomodoros, total focus time, tasks finished, and daily session history. You can review weekly trends and identify your most productive times — all without any manual data entry.
