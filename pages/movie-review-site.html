Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Movie Review Site template free to use?
Yes. You can start building your film review platform for free on MeDo — no credit card or coding skills required. MeDo handles hosting and deployment automatically, so there are no server costs to get started.
Can visitors write and publish reviews without creating an account?
The template supports both guest and registered user reviews. Through MeDo's AI interface you can configure whether anonymous submissions are allowed or require sign-in, giving you full control over your community's moderation approach.
How does the rating system work?
Each film entry displays an aggregate star rating calculated from all submitted reviews. Reviewers can score films on a configurable scale and leave written critiques. The system surfaces the highest-rated and most discussed films on the homepage automatically.
Can I customize the site to match my brand or niche?
Absolutely. Through MeDo's AI conversation interface you can change colors, fonts, category labels, and overall layout to match your niche — whether you focus on classic cinema, indie films, horror, or international releases.
