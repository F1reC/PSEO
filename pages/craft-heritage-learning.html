Frequently Asked Questions

Is the CraftHeritage Japan template free to use?
Yes. You can start building your cultural heritage learning platform for free on MeDo — no credit card or coding skills required. MeDo handles hosting and deployment automatically, so there are no server costs to get started.
What kinds of Japanese crafts are covered in the platform?
The platform covers a broad range of traditional Japanese crafts including pottery (yakimono), washi papermaking, lacquerware (urushi), textile weaving, origami, and woodworking. Each craft module includes video masterclasses, cultural context lessons, and a recommended materials list.
Can I host live workshops through this platform?
Yes. The live workshop feature allows you to schedule and stream interactive sessions with artisans. Participants can ask questions in real time, and sessions can be recorded for on-demand replay in the course library.
Can I customize the platform to feature specific crafts or regions?
Absolutely. Through MeDo's AI conversation interface you can tailor the craft catalog, region highlights, artisan profiles, and cultural lesson content to match your audience. Add your own branding and featured artisans without writing any code.
