Can I assign tasks to team members?
Yes. The template includes task assignment features where you can create tasks, assign them to team members, set due dates, and track completion status. Each team member can see their assigned work in a personal dashboard.
How does the Kanban board work?
The Kanban board lets you visualize your workflow with customizable columns like To Do, In Progress, and Done. Drag and drop tasks between columns, add labels and priorities, and see your entire project at a glance.
Can I customize this template for my specific workflow?
Absolutely. The template is fully customizable through MeDo's AI conversation interface. You can add custom fields, change board layouts, create automation rules, and tailor the tool to match any project methodology — Agile, Scrum, Waterfall, or your own.
