Yes. You can start building your cafe billing app for free on MeDo — no credit card or coding skills required. MeDo handles hosting and deployment automatically, so there are no server costs to get started.

Absolutely. The template includes a menu management module where you can add, edit, or remove items — including drinks, snacks, and seasonal specials — along with pricing and categories, all without touching code.
Does it support tax calculation and receipt printing?
Yes. The billing app automatically calculates applicable taxes based on your configuration and generates digital or printable receipts. You can customize receipt layouts, include your cafe name, and set GST or VAT rates.
