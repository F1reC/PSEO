Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Language Learning App template free to use?
Yes. You can start building with this template for free on MeDo — no credit card or coding skills required. MeDo handles hosting and deployment automatically, so there are no server costs to get started.
How does AI conversation practice work?
The AI acts as a conversation partner that adapts to your proficiency level. It engages you in realistic dialogues, corrects mistakes in real time, suggests better phrasing, and gradually increases complexity as you improve — just like practicing with a native speaker.
Can I customize this template for different languages?
Absolutely. The template is fully customizable through MeDo's AI conversation interface. You can configure it for any language — Spanish, French, Japanese, Mandarin, Arabic, and more — and tailor vocabulary lists, grammar rules, and cultural content to your target audience.
Can I track my language learning progress?
Yes. The app includes a built-in progress dashboard that tracks vocabulary mastery, conversation fluency scores, grammar accuracy, and daily streaks. Visual charts show your improvement over time across all language skills.
