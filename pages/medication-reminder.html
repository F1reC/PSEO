Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Medication Reminder template free to use?
Yes. You can start building your medication reminder app for free on MeDo. No credit card or technical skills required — MeDo handles hosting and notifications automatically.
Can multiple family members share one medication schedule?
Yes. The family sharing feature lets caregivers and family members view and manage medication schedules for loved ones. Each person can have their own profile with separate medications, doses, and reminder times — all in one shared app.
How do refill alerts work?
The app tracks your remaining pill count based on the dose schedule you enter. When supply drops below a configurable threshold, it sends a push notification reminding you to request a refill — so you never run out unexpectedly.
Can I log notes from doctor appointments?
Yes. The doctor notes feature lets you record instructions, dosage changes, and appointment summaries alongside each medication. Notes are organized by date and medication, making it easy to share your history with a new healthcare provider.
