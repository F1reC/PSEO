Book Listings

Members add books with title, author, edition, and photos. Search and filter the catalog by genre, language, condition, and location to find the right titles fast.

Exchange Matching

The platform compares want lists against available books and surfaces compatible matches. Both readers are notified when their lists align — no manual browsing required.

Reading Lists

Members track books across three shelves — want to read, currently reading, and finished. Personalized lists make it easy to manage a reading life and discover what to pick up next.

Community Reviews

Readers write honest reviews and star ratings for books they've finished. Reviews help others decide what to request and build a trustworthy reputation system around listings.

Location-Based Search

Filter listings by distance to find books available nearby. Coordinate local pickups and reduce the friction of mailing — especially useful for large or heavy titles.

Condition Rating

Every listing includes a standardized condition rating — from like new to well-loved. Buyers set their minimum acceptable condition so expectations are clear before any exchange.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the BookShare platform template free to use?
Yes. You can start building your book exchange platform for free on MeDo — no credit card or coding skills required. MeDo handles hosting and deployment automatically so your community can start sharing books immediately.
How does the exchange matching work?
The exchange matching feature compares books in your members' want lists against available listings. When a match is found, both parties are notified and can arrange the exchange — making it easy to find specific titles without manual searching.
Can members search for books near them?
Yes. The location-based search lets members filter listings by proximity, making local pickup exchanges easy to coordinate. This is especially useful for book clubs, university campuses, and neighborhood reading communities.
Does it support community reviews and reading lists?
Yes. Members can write reviews, rate books, and build personal reading lists — want to read, currently reading, and finished. Community reviews help others discover books worth picking up and build trust around listings.
