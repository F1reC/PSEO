Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Habit Tracker App template free to use?
Yes. You can start building your habit tracker for free on MeDo — no credit card or coding skills required. MeDo handles hosting and deployment automatically, so there are no server costs to get started.
How does streak tracking work?
The streak counter increments every day you check off all your tracked habits. Break the chain by missing a day and the counter resets — this simple mechanic creates powerful psychological motivation to maintain consistent behavior over time.
Does the app store data locally or in the cloud?
The template uses local data storage by default, so habit data is saved directly in the user's browser without requiring an account or backend. Through MeDo's AI interface you can add cloud sync or user authentication if needed.
Can I customize which habits to track?
Absolutely. Add any habits you want to track, set custom labels, assign colors or icons, and configure daily or weekly frequency goals. The Notion-style interface makes adding and managing habits intuitive and flexible.
